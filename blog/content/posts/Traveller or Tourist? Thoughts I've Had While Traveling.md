The date is August 21st, 2026. I'm currently sitting in a coffee shop in Phong Nha writing this. Out the window is a stretch of road in this intimate little town where most of the tourists congregate. It's literally just a strip. And on either side you can find restaurants, pharmacies and grocery stores. And of course hotels and hostels to house the people coming in and out. I guess it's the economic backbone of this place which makes sense as it's a tourist attraction rather than a place that people live.

Earlier today I took an easy rider and got maybe a better idea of what life is really like here. Aside from the towering limestone karst structures that had me in awe I saw the occasional house either right next to the road or far off into the distance. These homes are humble and I can get some sort of access to how people maybe really live here by just looking at them. But all the more I am here the more that I truly realize that I am just a tourist after all.

I try not to be I try to be more 'authentic' and in ways I have certainly succeeded. But the true life in Vietnam is separate to what I've experienced in a distinct way. I've gotten a taste but I know there's so much more. There are some limitations that prevent me from diving deeper. Number one is language: This is obvious since without communication I can't truly connect on a deeper level to the locals. Second is my reliance on buses to get around and public transport. Sure I am backpacking and in that way I'm mobile, however, I must learn to ride a scooter or motorbike something that will allow me to go where I truly want and to go off the beaten path to truly see things that feel more authentic. Third is probably just a little more confidence and courage.

These are just some things that I've noticed. I don't expect to become truly 'local' I have my own identity but I think that next time I do something like this I should have the mentality more so of just whipping out my phone looking on a map and picking a place not because I was told to by some person on the internet but because I thought it looked cool. Think more for myself and follow my intuition.

A part of me FEELS like a tourist but I've definitely felt like a traveller to. More on that in another post :)
