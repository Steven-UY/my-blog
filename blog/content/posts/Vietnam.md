### Da Lat

![[Pasted image 20260724093223.png]]

Dalat looks somewhat like a cross between Vietnam and rural France. Many of its hotels and houses are built in a European/French style. The city spreads across a series of pine-covered hills, with a small lake in the centre and surrounded by a man-made lake and higher peaks, making for some lovely scenery quite different from the rest of Vietnam. 2 days here. Kinda mountainous not as much to do but just see it ig.
